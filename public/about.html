My journey

Hello, I'm Ash

My mother is a tarot reader, and watching her is how I first fell in love with the cards. I began practising on my own, then studied formally and became a certified tarot reader. That was more than ten years ago, and I've been reading ever since.

I work with more than five decks, letting guidance choose the right one for each reading. Most often I reach for my Rider-Waite tarot deck.

Style

Intuitive & gentle

Readings are guided, relaxed and gentle, a calm space to explore what's on your mind.

Practice

Tarot, oracle & energy

Tarot and oracle card readings, alongside energy work and guidance.

Gratitude

With thanks

To my mom, who showed me the cards, and to my teacher, whose course gave me my certification.

Who I read for

Anyone seeking guidance on career, love, a yes-or-no question, a move to a new home, work, or the months and year ahead. I welcome clients from every city and country.

I don't read on death or hospitalisation, and I don't read for anyone under 18. Readings booked just for fun, without a real question, aren't something I take on.
