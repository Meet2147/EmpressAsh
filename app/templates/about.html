About me

I'm Ash

Reader of tarot, oracle cards and runes, and a guide for those standing at a crossroads.

✦

How I found the cards

My mother is a tarot reader, and watching her shuffle and lay out the cards is how I first fell in love with them. I began practising on my own, and over the years it became the way I listen to people and help them see their path more clearly. That was more than ten years ago, and I've been reading ever since.

I'm also a certified tarot reader, with thanks to my mom and to the teacher whose course I took.
