Policies & disclaimer

Disclaimer

All readings are intended for spiritual reflection and personal guidance. Tarot interpretations are subjective and do not guarantee outcomes. They are not a substitute for medical, legal, mental health, property or professional financial advice.

Age

Clients must be 18 or older. Ash does not read for anyone under 18.

Boundaries

  • No readings on death or hospitalisation.
  • Please keep questions relevant and to the point, and don't ask Ash personal questions about herself or her whereabouts.
  • Readings booked only for fun or curiosity, without a genuine question, are not accepted.

Booking & payment

  • Appointments are confirmed upon full payment.
  • Live readings must be booked at least one week in advance.
  • Ash needs your full real name and your question before the reading.
  • Written summaries and written readings are delivered within 48 hours.

Cancellations, rescheduling & refunds

Ash's cancellation, rescheduling, no-show and refund terms will appear here.

Confidentiality & recordings

All sessions are confidential. Sessions are not recorded, by Ash or by the client; your written report arrives within 48 hours instead.

Your details & privacy

When you book or join, Ash keeps your name, email, mobile number, date of birth and question. They are used to deliver your reading, send your birthday gift and share occasional offers. Your details are never sold. To have them removed, message Ash and they will be deleted.
