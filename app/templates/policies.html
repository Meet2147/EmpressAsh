Policies & disclaimer

Disclaimer

All readings are intended for spiritual reflection and personal guidance. Tarot interpretations are subjective and do not guarantee outcomes. They are not a substitute for medical, legal, mental health, property or professional financial advice.

Age

Clients must be 18 or older. Ash does not read for anyone under 18.

Boundaries

  • No readings on death or hospitalisation.
  • Please keep questions relevant and to the point, and don't ask Ash personal questions about herself or her whereabouts.
  • Readings booked only for fun or curiosity, without a genuine question, are not accepted.

Booking & payment

  • Appointments are confirmed upon full payment.
  • Live readings must be booked at least one week in advance.
  • Ash needs your full real name and your question before the reading.
  • Written summaries and written readings are delivered within 48 hours.

Conduct on live calls and messages

Video and voice readings are one-to-one. To keep them safe and respectful for everyone, these rules apply to every call, message, comment and review on EmpressAsh.

  • No hate speech. Nothing that attacks or demeans anyone for their religion, caste, race, ethnicity, nationality, gender, sexual orientation, disability or age.
  • No sexual remarks or requests. No flirting, sexual comments, innuendo or requests for photos, personal contact or meetings.
  • No nudity or sexual content. Be fully dressed and in a private, appropriate place with your camera on you only. Nothing sexual or explicit may be shown, sent or shared.
  • No harassment, threats or abuse, including swearing at Ash, intimidation, or contacting her outside EmpressAsh.
  • No recording, screenshots or screen capture of a session, by either side, and no sharing of session links.
  • You must be the person who booked, aged 18 or over. No one else may join without Ash's agreement.
  • No drugs or alcohol during a session, and no illegal requests (for example, to harm or control another person).

What happens if the rules are broken: Ash will end the call or conversation immediately. A session ended for a breach is not refunded. Ash may block the person from booking, joining or messaging again, and serious matters such as threats or explicit content may be reported to the platform involved and to the police.

Reporting: if anything on a call or in a message makes you uncomfortable, use Report a problem in your private reading room, or contact Ash. Reports are private.

Comments and reviews that contain hateful or sexual language are held back until Ash checks them, and may be removed.

Cancellations, rescheduling & refunds

Ash's cancellation, rescheduling, no-show and refund terms will appear here.

Confidentiality & recordings

All sessions are confidential. Sessions are not recorded, by Ash or by the client; your written report arrives within 48 hours instead.

Your details & privacy

When you book or join, Ash keeps your name, email, mobile number, date of birth and question. They are used to deliver your reading, send your birthday gift and share occasional offers. Your details are never sold. To have them removed, message Ash and they will be deleted.
